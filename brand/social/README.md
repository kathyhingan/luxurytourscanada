# Luxury Tours Canada social kit

Everything here is built from the brand spec: the custom maple mark (gradient
Maple Red #B3202A through Burnt Orange #C4622D to Harvest Gold #E3B04B),
Deep Spruce #10281F background, wordmark in Cormorant Garamond, sub-line in
Montserrat. All profiles are the same artwork at each platform's exact size,
and every design is safe inside circular crops.

### Platforms with no banner

Instagram, TikTok, Pinterest, Threads, Yelp and Tripadvisor do not offer a
profile banner or cover image. On those, the profile mark is the whole
presence, and the Instagram bio is where the "Private Journeys, Coast to
Coast" line belongs.

## Note on the mark

The leaf is a custom drawing: softened notches and a slimmer stem than the
national flag leaf, with the brand gradient. It is deliberately not the
eleven point government symbol. Before any trademark filing, have a Canadian
trademark lawyer review it, as the brand document advises.
