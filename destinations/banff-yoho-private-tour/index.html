The region

The famous lakes, at the right hour.

Banff and Lake Louise are the most photographed places in Canada, and in July they feel like it. We do not refuse them. We take guests at dawn, in shoulder season, or by routes that bypass the queues, and we treat them as a chapter inside a Rockies journey rather than the whole book. The reward is the lake at sunrise with the mist still on it, and a shoreline with three walkers on it instead of three hundred.

Yoho is the quieter neighbour, half an hour west, with Emerald Lake, Takakkaw Falls, and roads where you hear the river before you see it. Access rules around Lake Louise and Moraine Lake have changed materially in recent years, so we confirm the current system before every journey and build the day around it rather than around last year's instructions.

Planning notes

What to know before you go

Getting there

Fly to Calgary, and the park gate is ninety minutes west. Your guide and private vehicle are with you from the airport, so the parkway is never a transfer, it is the point.

Best season

Mid September to early October for golden larches, elk, and room at every viewpoint. Late June to mid September for everything open and long light. Winter brings skiing, ice walks, and frozen lakes, with far fewer people on the parkway.

The Icefields Parkway

232 kilometres between Lake Louise and Jasper with very few services. We fill the tank before we start and carry what the day needs, because the next fuel stop can be an hour behind you.

Access rules

The Moraine Lake road closed to private vehicles in 2023, and parking at Lake Louise is managed. We confirm the current rules with Parks Canada before every journey and build the day around the system that exists, not the one from memory.

Altitude and pacing

Lake Louise sits near 1,600 metres and the passes climb higher. We build a gentle first day at altitude for every guest, and the itinerary states the walking honestly for each stop.

Pacing and care

One base, one vehicle, the same guide throughout, and no departure before half past eight. Banff has a hospital and the park keeps year-round warden coverage, and travel medical insurance is essential in this country, so we say so in writing.

How we design this journey

Checked before every journey, not assumed

The Rockies change their own rules: the Moraine Lake road, the parking systems at Lake Louise, the season dates for the Yoho falls road, and the recovery work still under way after the 2024 wildfire season. We check the current status of every road and walk with Parks Canada before we quote, and we design the day around what is true this month.

Everything here has been done first: the lake before the boats, the viewpoint before the sun clears the ridge, the falls before the midday traffic. Where a rule or a weather system changes the plan, the fallback is already written: a different valley at the same hour, and a second location for any morning we have promised on the water.

Questions we are asked

Frequently asked questions

What is the Icefields Parkway and how long does it take?

It is the 232 kilometre drive between Lake Louise and Jasper, one of the great mountain roads on earth. Driven straight through it takes about three and a half hours; done properly with stops at the glaciers, waterfalls, and viewpoints, it is a full day or two. We drive it slowly, and we fill the tank before we start, because services along it are very limited.

How do you avoid the crowds at Lake Louise and Moraine Lake?

Timing and access planning. We arrive at sunrise or in shoulder season, and we check the current road and parking rules before every journey, because they have changed in recent years. Where access is restricted, we build the day around the current system instead of a route that no longer works.

Is Yoho worth adding to a Banff journey?

Yoho is what Banff was before the world found it: Emerald Lake, Takakkaw Falls, and quiet roads, half an hour from Lake Louise. We include it in most Rockies journeys, and for guests who want the scenery without the volume, we lead with it.
