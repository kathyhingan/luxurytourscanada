The island

The largest Gulf Island runs on farm time.

Cheese, cider, lamb, lavender, and a Saturday market that has run on the Ganges waterfront for decades. The whole island is small enough to see properly in three days, and every drive runs minutes, not hours. It is the counterweight to everything busy about the west coast, and the leg of a journey where people finally stop checking the time.

You arrive by floatplane from Vancouver or by ferry with the vehicle on deck, and the island does the rest. Our retirees call this the leg where they exhale. Our couples book it for the same reason, and autumn, when the stalls fill with harvest and the roads empty out, is the best of it.

Planning notes

What to know before you go

Getting there

A scheduled floatplane from Vancouver takes about thirty five minutes, or the ferry crosses with the private vehicle on deck. Either way, you land with the car and the guide already in place.

Best season

May to October for the market season. September and October bring the harvest and the empty roads. Winter is quiet to the point of empty, and we build it only for guests who want exactly that.

The Saturday market

Runs roughly April to October and sets the shape of the stay. We anchor one Saturday on it and plan the other days around it, never the reverse.

How long

Two to four nights. One market morning, one lake or orchard day, and one day with nothing booked, which on this island is the point.

Getting around

One base, one private vehicle, the same guide throughout. Drives run minutes, so the days flex around weather and appetite instead of a timetable.

Pacing and care

Flat ground, short walks, and no departure before half past eight. The nearest hospital is on the island, minutes from the harbour, and we note it in every itinerary.

How we design this journey

Built around a market morning

Island time is the point, so the Saturday market anchors the stay and everything else is planned around it. One base, one vehicle, the same guide from floatplane to farewell, and no day that starts before breakfast.

Everything here has been done first: the market before the crowd, the chairs at the Maxwell lookout, the table where the food was grown in sight of the plates. If a producer sells to the island first and visitors second, that is where we eat.

Frequently asked questions

How do you get to Salt Spring Island?

By scheduled floatplane from Vancouver, roughly thirty five minutes, or by ferry with the private vehicle on deck. The floatplane is the more memorable arrival and the one we build first for most journeys, because the island opens up below you before you land.

Is Salt Spring Island suited to travellers who prefer a slower pace?

It is the best leg in our portfolio for exactly that. Short drives, flat walking, one base, and a day that can be rearranged at breakfast without losing anything. We hold the same vehicle and the same guide for the whole stay.

What can we do on Salt Spring with children?

Farm visits, the market, lake swimming at Cusheon in summer, and short walks with a guide. Families do well here when the days stay short, because the island's distances make a relaxed rhythm easy to keep.
