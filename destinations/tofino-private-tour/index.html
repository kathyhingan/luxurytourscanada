The coast

Tofino has been discovered. The water has not.

In July and August the main beaches know it. So we do not build summer journeys around Chesterman Beach at midday. We build them around the hours and the waters that stay quiet: dawn, the shoulder seasons, and the outer reaches of Clayoquot Sound, reached by private boat with a naturalist who knows where the whales have been feeding that week.

Every private Tofino tour we build starts on the water. What this coast does best is found in May and June, from September into October, and through the winter, when the Pacific puts on a show and almost nobody is there to see it. The road in from Nanaimo takes roughly three and a half to four hours, and it is one of the finest drives on the coast. A floatplane is the other way in, so the journey can begin above the water.

Planning notes

What to know before you go

Getting there

The Pacific Rim Highway takes three and a half to four hours from Nanaimo and deserves a full day with stops. The other way in is a floatplane straight into the harbour.

Best season

May and June bring grey whales, long evenings, and empty beaches. September and October bring the clearest light of the year and the whale watching most people miss.

The crowd problem

July and August are the busy weeks on this coast, workable only with dawn starts and a boat in the outer waters. We plan them honestly or steer you to another window.

How long

Three to five nights. Two boat days, one land day, and one weather day, because boat and floatplane days here are weather dependent.

Storm season

November to February: big swell, lodges built around the view, and the quietest the coast ever gets. Dress for rain and you will love it.

Getting around

A private vehicle stays with you for the land days, and the boat days start from the harbour. Nothing depends on a shuttle timetable.

How we design this journey

Timed to the water, not the brochure

We build the boat days first, because the water decides everything else, and then shape the land days around the weather window. Your guide checks the marine forecast the evening before, and the fallback day is already written before you fly.

Everything in the plan has been done here first: the soak at Hot Springs Cove after the boats leave, the walk on Meares Island at the hour the light comes through the canopy, the table at the end of the day where the fishing boats are still coming in.

Frequently asked questions

When is the best time for a private Tofino tour?

May and June, then September and October, then the storm season from November to February. July and August are the busiest weeks on this coast, and we work them only with dawn starts and a boat in the outer waters.

Can you see whales and bears on a private Tofino boat day?

Yes, with a naturalist guide and a private vessel, and always at a respectful distance. Grey whales pass through in spring, humpbacks feed offshore through the summer and autumn, and black bears work the low tide line in the sound. We time the boat day to the tide, not to a fixed departure.

Is Tofino worth visiting in winter?

For storm watching, yes, and it is the quietest the coast ever gets. Expect rain, big swell, and lodges built around the view. We book the room with the window and keep the walking short and the fire close.
