Corporate and MICE

Canada Corporate Retreat Luxury: Impressive, Seamless, Nothing Goes Wrong

One point of contact from brief to debrief. Private mountain and coastal retreats for six to thirty, with a fallback venue for every programme day and a transport manifest for every transfer.

Schedule A 30 Minute Brief

When a leadership team travels, one fear runs underneath everything: something going wrong on the organiser's watch, in front of the chief executive or the client. So we start from the opposite end to a leisure journey and work outward from execution. One point of contact, one plan, and a written contingency for weather, closures, and wildfire smoke at every stage.

What the group remembers afterwards is the part we do not compromise: a private dinner in a place no group has dined, a mountain morning that resets a room full of people, and a programme that leaves the team closer than any conference hotel could manage.

Programme overview

Three to five days, structured around outcomes

Day one: arrival and reset

Private transfers from Calgary, Vancouver, or Toronto, a briefing on the ground, and an evening that gets everyone out of the airport mindset.

Day two: the work, in a room worth sitting in

Morning sessions with AV support for hybrid attendees, an outdoor afternoon that changes the conversation, and dinner together.

Day three: the thing they will talk about

A private guided day, a dinner in a place no group has dined, and reflection built into the evening rather than left to the flight home.

Logistics assurance

What the organiser needs in writing

  • One point of contact from brief to debrief, reachable throughout
  • A fallback venue and sequence for weather, closures, or wildfire smoke
  • Airport-to-lodge private transfers from Calgary, Vancouver, or Toronto
  • Transport manifests for every movement of the group
  • AV support arranged for hybrid sessions, tested before the group arrives
  • Multi-day mountain and coastal retreats for groups up to thirty
What this delivers

Outcomes, stated before experiences

Team cohesion earned in a canoe rather than a conference room. Leadership alignment reached over a long table at the end of a day that took everyone out of their inbox. And cultural capital: the retelling that raises how the team talks about where it works and who it works with. We will bring the value argument against Whistler, Aspen, or the Alps to the briefing, checked at the current exchange rate rather than asserted.

Investment

Retreats of this scope are quoted per programme, after a short brief, because the variables are the group size, the season, and the properties. We will give you a range at the briefing and a precise figure once the shape is settled. No price grids, ever.

Questions we are asked

Frequently asked questions

What group sizes can you handle for a corporate retreat in Canada?

Six to thirty guests, across multi-day mountain and coastal retreats. Banff and Lake Louise handle larger groups comfortably, and the BC coast suits smaller leadership groups where the point is space rather than scale.

What happens if weather or wildfire smoke disrupts the programme?

We write a fallback venue and a fallback sequence into every programme before you travel, so a disruption changes the plan rather than the outcome. Wildfire season runs July to September, and it is planned around rather than hoped away.

Do you support hybrid sessions for remote attendees?

Yes. AV support is arranged for hybrid sessions and tested before the group arrives. We will tell you plainly what the venue can and cannot do rather than promising a seamless link we have not verified.
