Families with children

Luxury Family Tours Canada: Travel With Kids, Done Right

Car seats in every vehicle, drives timed around naps, wildlife briefings before every walk, and a beach plan that respects how cold the Pacific actually is.

Plan A Family Journey

Why we understand family travel
We have children ourselves, so we know what it actually takes to cross this country with them: the logistics that fall apart, the schedules that do not survive contact with a six year old, and the moments that make all of it worth it.

Our own family does not travel on client journeys. Local guides and vetted childminders look after the family-facing details on the ground, and every family journey is designed around that reality rather than around the version of family travel that looks good in a brochure.

What we do differently

The things nobody else thinks about

Car seats, pre-sourced

Child seats are required by law in Canada, and on a long drive they are essential. We confirm the right seats for each child before you land, in every vehicle.

Wildlife briefings for children

Short, clear, and repeated before every walk, so the wildlife stays at a distance and the children understand why. Bears are seen, never approached.

Cold water, taken seriously

Pacific and glacial water is cold year round. Wetsuits for any swimming, guide-supervised beach days only, and the lake rather than the ocean when the children want to stay in.

By age

What works at each age

  • Toddlers, 0 to 3. Car seats, nap-timed drives, warm layers, and shallow beach play only. Salt Spring's short distances suit this stage better than the Rockies.
  • Young children, 4 to 8. Wildlife, canoeing, farm days on Salt Spring, and energy managed rather than spent. Long drives get stops built in.
  • Pre-teens, 9 to 14. Glacier walks, canoeing, and the cultural experiences they will actually remember. The Rockies open up properly at this age.
What is included

Everything pre-sourced before you land

  • Child seats pre-sourced for every vehicle, and fitted before your arrival
  • A cot, high chair, and carriers arranged at each property, confirmed in advance
  • Wetsuits and child-sized safety equipment for water days
  • Wildlife briefings for children before every walk
  • Vetted childminder contacts at each destination, on request
  • Private vehicles with nap-timed drives and stops built into every long leg
  • A sick-day fallback written into the itinerary, because children are children
  • A 24-hour contact for the whole journey

Not included: international flights, travel medical insurance (strongly recommended for every traveller), eTA fees where required, personal shopping, and gratuities.

Questions we are asked

Frequently asked questions

What is the best Canada itinerary with a toddler?

Short distances, one base, and a gentle rhythm. Salt Spring Island and the Ontario lakeshore work better for toddlers than the Rockies, because the drives are fifteen minutes rather than three hours. If the Rockies are the dream, we build it around Jasper with nap-timed drives and a rest in the middle of each day.

Do you provide car seats for children in Canada?

Yes. Child seats are required by law, and we pre-source the correct seats for each child in every vehicle, fitted before you arrive. It is the first line of our pre-arrival checklist and not something you need to bring.

Is it safe to travel in bear country with children?

It is, with the right briefings. Wildlife is viewed at distance and guide-led, never approached, and every walk starts with a short briefing pitched at the children. Bear spray, group positioning, and route choice are the guide's job, and they are done every day.
