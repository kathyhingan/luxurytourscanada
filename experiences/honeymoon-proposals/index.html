The approach

Honeymoons and proposals run on feeling first.

These are the two journeys where feeling comes first and logistics come second, and both are the happier for it. You tell us the moment you want, and we build the days around it: private lodges, quiet water, dinners at tables no one else is at, and the small logistics handled so completely that you never have to think about them.

For proposal planners, everything runs quietly in the background. A hidden photographer, a private location, a decoy activity, and a second location and time window, because mountain weather turns by the hour and this moment does not get a second take.

Planning notes

What to know before you go

When to travel

May and June, then September and October, for the BC coast and its storms. Late June to early October for the Rockies. Tell us your month and we will tell you honestly which setting pairs best with it.

How long

Five to eight days for a honeymoon in one region, done slowly. Ten to fourteen to pair the Pacific coast with the mountains. The trip should feel shorter than it is.

Where to base

Two bases at most, chosen so you unpack once and settle rather than move every night. A lodge with a view beats a list of towns ticked off.

The proposal

One person plans it, correspondence stays discreet, and the photographer, location, and decoy activity are arranged before you land. Nothing about the day reads as staged.

The weather

Mountain weather turns by the hour and the Pacific keeps its own forecast. Every outdoor day carries a fallback, and your planner watches the forecast the evening before, not the morning of.

The quiet

No group departures, no shared boats, and a schedule that starts when you want it to. If you would rather stay in with the view, the day can be left empty on purpose.

  • Private transfers throughout, including the arrival, so the journey starts calmly
  • A private boat day on the coast, or a canoe on a mountain lake, guide included
  • The proposal setup on a proposal journey: location, timing, photographer, decoy, and the fallback
  • Private dinners and one table arranged where the setting does the work
  • Accommodation at the finest available property in each setting, confirmed after our call
  • A single point of contact before you travel, and a 24-hour line while you are in the country

Not included: international flights, alcohol outside arranged dinners, personal shopping, activities added outside the plan, travel medical insurance, and gratuities.

How we plan it

The details stay out of your way

A honeymoon should not feel managed, even when it is. We build the day around the two or three moments that matter, then handle everything around them quietly: the transfer timed to your flight, the table held under one name, the boat booked before the harbour fills, and the weather watched from the evening before.

For a proposal, that work happens weeks ahead and stays invisible. The location is checked at the same hour on the same day of the week in the same season, the photographer is positioned so the moment is captured without being seen, and every written fallback is rehearsed with you. Nothing is left to chance, and nothing about it feels arranged.

Questions we are asked

Frequently asked questions

Where in Canada is best for a honeymoon?

Tofino for the Pacific, the storms, and the room with the tub. Salt Spring Island for slowness and farm tables. Jasper for dark skies and mountain lodges. The best honeymoon pairs two of them, and the season decides which pair works.

Can you plan a surprise proposal without my partner finding out?

Yes, and it is a large part of what we do. The planning runs through one person, the correspondence is discreet, the photographer and location are arranged in advance, and the decoy activity is built so the day reads as an ordinary one right up to the moment.

What if the weather ruins the proposal location?

Every proposal we plan carries a second location and a second time window at the same hour, plus a written plan for what could go wrong. Mountain weather turns by the hour, so this is not pessimism, it is how a proposal gets made on the day you planned it.
