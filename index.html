Luxury Tours Canada: Journeys the Highway Never Shows You

You have seen Banff from a hundred photographs. We design the other Canada: mountains at dawn, an island that still runs on farm stands, and a road with no one on it at six in the morning.

Begin Your Journey

Luxury Tours Canada designs private, multi-destination journeys across one of the largest countries on earth. Every journey is built from scratch around one party, a maximum of eight guests, with private vehicles, private boats, and guides who live where they work.

We work from 27 years of travelling this country the slow way: the Rockies, the BC coast, Salt Spring Island, Tofino, Jasper, Banff, and the Ontario lakeshore from Kingston to Essex. That knowledge is the product. Distance is part of the design, wildfire season is planned around months ahead, and mountain weather that turns by the hour gets a second plan for the same day, every day.

Why we understand Canada
Six crossings of this country by thumb before I owned a passport stamp, and 700 kilometres by bicycle from Kingston to Essex. I did it the hard way, so I know exactly where the hard way stops being worth it.

Our credibility: 27 years of personal journeys across Canada, beginning at sixteen with a thumb out on the Trans-Canada Highway. Every route we design has been driven, walked, or paddled by someone who lives here.

What we do differently

Four things a group tour cannot give you

Jasper first, Banff at dawn

Banff and Lake Louise are thoroughly discovered. We take clients at sunrise, in shoulder season, or by routes that bypass the queues, and we build most Rockies journeys around Jasper, which is larger, quieter, and holds one of the largest dark-sky preserves on earth.

Eight guests, never more

No coaches, no strangers, no fixed departures. Your party travels in a private vehicle with a guide who knows which pull-off has the view and which one has the bears.

Weather planned for, not apologised for

Mountain weather turns by the hour and July to September carries wildfire smoke. Every outdoor day carries a fallback in the same day, and every summer proposal carries an alternative location.

One conversation, one designer

You tell us once what matters: pace, wildlife, wine, altitude, children, a proposal. The journey is then designed around that, and adjusted while you travel.

How it works

Three steps, then a journey

  1. 1
    Tell us what you are imagining. Dates, season, the people travelling, and what would make the trip worth it. Twenty minutes is usually enough.
  2. 2
    We design it around you. A day-by-day journey with drive times stated honestly, private vehicles and boats, and a fallback for every outdoor day.
  3. 3
    You travel, we stay on call. One point of contact for the whole journey, and a 24-hour line while you are in the country.
What is included

The logistics you will not have to think about

  • All private ground transfers, airport to lodge and between destinations
  • Domestic flights and floatplane transfers where the journey calls for them
  • Private boat, canoe, and crew on every water day
  • A dedicated local guide throughout, not a driver with a script
  • Daily breakfast, plus the lunches and dinners noted in your itinerary
  • National and provincial park passes, and all entrance fees
  • Wildlife-safety briefings before every walk
  • Accommodation at the finest available property in each location, confirmed after our call
  • A 24-hour Luxury Tours Canada contact for the whole journey

Not included: international flights, alcoholic beverages and room charges, personal shopping, activities added outside the itinerary, travel medical insurance (strongly recommended, and essential for retirees), eTA fees where required, and gratuities.
