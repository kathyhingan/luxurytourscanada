Our Philosophy

Every client we serve is paying to not think about logistics.

We are not a booking engine. We are not an operator with fixed packages. Luxury Tours Canada is a small, expert team that has spent decades learning which roads are worth the day, which pull-off has the view and which one has the bears, and which seasons reward each coast and each pass.

We build your journey from scratch, whether you are newly married and planning the honeymoon of your lives, travelling with children who need more than a gift shop, a retiree finally taking the private guided tour of Canada you have spent years imagining, or a corporate team that needs something genuinely extraordinary. Then we handle everything, so you can be entirely present for it.

How It Works

Four steps. One journey. Everything handled.

There are no packages here. Just a process designed to turn what you are imagining into something that exceeds it.

01
Tell Us What You Are Imagining

Take the five-question quiz above. Fill in the form below. Tell us as much or as little as you know. We do the rest.

02
We Respond Within 24 Hours

A real person reads every inquiry. Within 24 hours, you will have our initial thinking, destinations, pacing, and a few questions that help us go further.

03
We Design Your Journey

A bespoke day-by-day itinerary built entirely around your group. Not a template. Not a fixed package. A journey designed from scratch, revised until every element is right.

04
Your Journey Is Confirmed

We handle everything: vessels, transfers, lodges, permits, equipment, dietary requirements. You arrive. We have already thought of the rest.
