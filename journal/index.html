What we write about

The four kinds of guide you will find here

  • Season guides. What a region is genuinely like month by month, including the months we avoid and why. Jasper in September for the larches, Tofino in storm season, icewine in Niagara in January.
  • Crowd honesty. Which famous places earn their reputation, at which hours they stay quiet, and which ones we route around entirely.
  • Operational reality. Drive times between bases, where the services are, what wildfire season means for a summer plan, and why every outdoor day carries a fallback.
  • Segment reads. What the same region looks like for a family with a toddler, a retiree couple, and a leadership team, because they are three different trips.

Every guide ends the same way our journeys begin: with an invitation to a conversation rather than a shopping cart.

How to use these guides

Read the one that matches your month, not the one that matches your wish list. Canadian travel is planned ahead of the season, so a September journey is chosen in winter, a storm season journey on the Pacific is chosen in autumn, and a wine journey on the lakeshore is chosen before the harvest. If the guide for your month says the region is wrong for your dates, believe it: we would rather move your region than defend your dates.

When one of these guides sounds like your trip, the next step is a short conversation about the people travelling, the pace, and the season. That conversation is where the journey starts, and everything on this site is written to get you to it well informed.

Start with the destinations themselves: the Tofino private tour page, the Jasper private tour page, or the full destinations index.
